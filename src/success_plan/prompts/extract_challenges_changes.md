Task: list {company}'s key challenges, and its leadership or structural changes.

Sources: the 10-K Risk Factors section (Item 1A) for fiscal year {fiscal_year}, Form 8-K filings about
change events, recent news headlines, and the most recent earnings releases. The earnings releases are
here only to corroborate or update the other sources.

Challenges
- A challenge is a risk, pressure, or obstacle that could affect the company's priorities or its
  willingness to invest. Draw challenges from Risk Factors, 8-K filings, and news headlines.
- Do not draw a challenge from an earnings release alone. You may cite a release as extra support.
- Exclude analyst ratings, price targets, stock-price movements, and investor position changes entirely.
- Legal disputes may be challenges.
- Return at most 12 challenges.

Leadership and structural changes
- Changes in leadership roles, reorganizations, layoffs or restructurings, acquisitions, divestitures,
  and financing events.
- Write "the Chief Financial Officer role changed", not a person's name. Do not speculate about
  reasons, performance, or motives. State only what the source states.
- For a financing event, state only what the filing says. Do not attach a business purpose unless the
  filing states it.

News headlines are weak signals. If a headline's claim also appears in an 8-K, the 10-K, or an earnings
release, cite that filing on the same sentence as the headline.
